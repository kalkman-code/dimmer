# Design

## Surfaces

Dimmer has a compact menu-bar panel for live status and quick actions, plus one resizable Settings window for configuration. The settings window uses the dark veil body (`#141416`), raised grouped sections, native macOS typography and controls. It opens as a regular app and returns to accessory mode when closed. It cannot be minimised; its position and width are remembered and its height fits the available screen, with the Form scrolling vertically on smaller displays. Its minimum content size is 680 × 480 points, shared by the layout and NSWindow and maintained through hosting layout. The Form stays inside the window at that minimum and at larger widths, without horizontal scrolling. Localised descriptions wrap; the welcome scrolls within the available height. The panel is 320 points wide in every language.

## Feature boxes and lid bars

Settings is one box per feature, in the order a lid closes through them: Keyboard, Screen, Snap to blur, Blur by angle, then Privacy veil. Each box has a title row with its name in the lane colour, a live reading where relevant, its restore link and its own switch; controls appear only while that feature is on. A feature that is off is the title row alone, with "off" for its reading. Privacy veil is on by default; its shortcut and preview controls collapse with the box when switched off. Disabling it unregisters the shortcut and immediately clears an active shortcut veil without changing Snap to blur or Blur by angle. Snap tint affects only the snap veil. Privacy veil has its own blur strength and tint for the shortcut veil. Blur by angle has a separate Frost to Smoke control, applied in proportion to the angle blur's strength. A missing Privacy strength is seeded once from the saved Snap strength, preserving the previous shared look. Snap restore resets zone, snap distance, speed, strength and tint; Blur restore resets range and tint; Privacy veil restore resets its strength, Clear speed and shortcut tint, leaving its switch, Snap settings and shortcut assignment unchanged. The details line for Snap to blur and Blur by angle shows under the title while the box is open.

Every bar maps 0–130 degrees to its own horizontal scale with the same gutter, so the scales line up down the page; migrated endpoints are clamped to those limits. Keyboard and Screen use independent brightness ramps: warm lamp (`#E9C891`) and silver (`#E2E5EA`). Snap to blur is a flat moonlight band (`#B9C8E6`) for the zone. Blur by angle is lilac (`#C9B6E4`). The Frost to Smoke controls tint each box's veil without adding another angle bar. Filled endpoint handles have floating capsule tags for angle and brightness; each number becomes a borderless field while editing. Hover changes the cursor; handles retain their size. Drag near an endpoint to move it, or activate its number to type an exact value. The live lid position is a white line and angle capsule on every open bar. Screen control is handed back above its high endpoint.

Hairlines use `#2A2A30`; secondary labels use `#9A9EA7`. Wells use `#111113`, value tags `#232328`, axis ticks `#4A4D55` and controls `#6F84AD`. The screen ramp stops at its high endpoint with a vertical return mark. Restore links stay disabled at their defaults, drawn at the same 0.38 as dimmed rows, and keep their full words as their accessibility label.

## Snap to blur

The privacy veil appears immediately on every display after a sufficiently fast lid snap lands in the configured zone. Near misses show a partial veil that fades away automatically with its own short fade; input clears them using Clear speed, and Reduce Motion removes the fade. Reduce Transparency skips near misses and keeps a locked snap or shortcut as an opaque cover, removed immediately when cleared. Snap and Privacy veil have independent strength sliders; Privacy veil's existing preview uses its own strength and tint. Input clears a locked snap veil after its grace period; while Privacy veil is on, its shortcut has no grace period. Input and lifting the lid 3 degrees from its lowest position both use Clear speed (0–600 ms, in 10 ms steps; default 80 ms). Pausing, sleeping, going dark or quitting clears it immediately; disabling Snap clears a snap-triggered veil, leaving a shortcut veil alone. The full-screen windows ignore mouse events.

Each open blur box has a 300 × 64 pt neutral-page Preview, with the caption “A page, as the veil leaves it.” Snap uses its own strength and tint; Privacy veil uses only its own strength and tint, even with Snap off or no shortcut assigned. Blur by angle previews its own tint at full strength, independent of lid position and range. Each preview collapses with its own box and its VoiceOver label names that box. Strength and tint edits redraw only their own preview; zone edits leave all previews and the unrelated distance, speed and Clear speed controls unchanged. Each veil box has a compact Look menu immediately above its preview: Frost, Mist, Slate, Smoke and Charcoal. Snap and Privacy veil apply strength and tint; Blur by angle applies tint only. Manual edits show Custom, and applying a look is one undo step scoped to that box.

## Menu bar panel

The panel shows the current state, lid angle, keyboard level and screen level, with “Yours” when display control has been handed back. While paused for a set time the state reads “Paused until” and the time. After a manual keyboard change, “Resume keyboard dimming” sits directly under the state line it explains. When the screen is waiting for the lid, restoring or unavailable, one secondary line under the Screen readout says why; routine states stay silent. Keep awake is one pop-up menu of every preset (Off, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 5 hours, Indefinitely), plus “Until a time…” when that is the active choice; its deadline or error appears below. Go dark is directly available alongside Settings…, Pause/Resume, a “Pause for…” menu of 15 minutes and 1 hour (hidden while paused) and Quit Dimmer. More contains About, support, release and uninstall guidance.

## Interaction

Changes apply immediately, and Cmd+Z in Settings undoes a completed range, privacy change or switch change as one step; each box's restore link is undoable and never touches switches or shortcut assignments. Snap and angle blur share one veil window per screen. The stronger active strength sets blur; darkness is the maximum of each veil's strength-weighted tint, scaled by the stronger strength. The privacy shortcut uses its own strength and tint and works only while Privacy veil is on. Axis number controls use edit focus interactions and a custom focus ring so they can join the Tab loop with system Keyboard navigation off; live acceptance remains an integrator check. With any endpoint focused, left and right change its angle by one degree and up and down change brightness by five percentage points; for snap-zone endpoints, up and down change Snap strength by five points while left and right still change the angle. Exact typing retains normal caret behaviour. Locked privacy blur appears without animation; partial veils and clearing use short fades, disabled with Reduce Motion. Go dark is available after sensor detection succeeds or is unavailable. It captures current keyboard, display and gamma levels for its fade, regardless of lane switches or a keyboard yield; waking returns to ordinary lane behaviour and preserves the yield.

## Menu bar mark

Use the diamond template glyph for the dimming control, outlined while paused. Do not place text beside it; use its state to distinguish active from paused.
